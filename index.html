04 / What is VYRA?

NOT QUITE
OBJECT.
NOT QUITE
ORGANISM.

VYRA is an independent design project exploring the space where industrial design meets the unfamiliar. We create objects and forms that challenge the boundaries between utility, sculpture and identity.

Our language is dark, tactile and deliberately unexpected. We believe the future should not look like an imitation of the past.

A different form of belonging.
